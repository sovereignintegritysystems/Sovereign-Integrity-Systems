Platform

Sovereign Engines

Sovereign Engines is the principal technology and platform family beneath Sovereign Integrity Systems. Current work spans high-assurance Rust systems, deterministic and bounded execution, cryptography and post-quantum migration, evidence and provenance semantics, formal and adversarial assurance, resilient/offline operation and domain-specific systems.

The platform is developed with explicit separation between source, execution evidence, integration, effectiveness, independent validation and certification.

Assurance posture

SIS develops toward demanding assurance and regulated-system contexts, including Common Criteria / ISO/IEC 15408, DO-178C and DO-333, ISO 26262, IEC 61508, Canadian government security profiles and NIST post-quantum standards.

These are engineering, research and evidence-mapping contexts. They are not blanket claims of certification, accreditation, production authorization, independent validation or field effectiveness.
