01Institution

Canadian capability, retained authority and durable institutional knowledge.

SIS is being built as a Métis-founded, founder-led, Saskatchewan-rooted Canadian institution in formation. Its sovereignty objective is practical: increase Canada's capacity to originate, understand, operate, verify, repair and evolve consequential capabilities from first-party knowledge and source without making a foreign vendor, cloud, repository or proprietary platform the source of authority for core capability.

International science and collaboration remain compatible with that objective. SIS distinguishes cooperation from dependency: external systems may extend capability, while critical first-party knowledge, control, evidence and reconstruction paths should remain governable and recoverable in Canada.

A second founding objective is to expand first-class Métis and broader Indigenous participation in technical architecture, science and R&D, engineering, intellectual-property custody, finance, ownership and institutional decision-making—not merely downstream employment or symbolic inclusion.

02The Sovereign estate

Technology is one layer of a larger capability system.

SIS is designed so research, engineering, evidence, physical science, knowledge, relationships, commercialization and governance reinforce one another rather than becoming isolated silos.

01

R&D + Engineering

Sovereign R&D investigates questions, failures, external research and new directions. Sovereign Engineering turns admitted work into controlled implementation, tests, integration and evidence.

02

Evidence + assurance

Source, execution, integration, effectiveness, independent validation and certification remain separate evidence states. Claims stay bounded by what is actually observed.

03

Physical science

Materials characterization, spectroscopy, sensors, metrology, fire and harsh-environment work, vibroacoustics, structural dynamics and reproducible measurement extend the evidence discipline beyond software.

04

ANNA

The Adaptive Navigation Network Architecture is the human learning and navigation layer: ordinary conversation over governed institutional knowledge, without turning institutional learning into hidden personnel authority.

05

Worldbook

A structured map of companies, Crown corporations, governments, Indigenous institutions, universities, laboratories, suppliers, funders and other strategic actors—linked to capabilities, evidence, dependencies and opportunities rather than maintained as a contact list.

06

Commercial + external

07

Institutional operations

08

03Platform

Sovereign Engines

Sovereign Engines is the principal technology and platform family beneath Sovereign Integrity Systems. Current work spans high-assurance Rust systems, deterministic and bounded execution, cryptography and post-quantum migration, evidence and provenance semantics, formal and adversarial assurance, resilient/offline operation and domain-specific research systems.

The platform separates source, execution evidence, integration, effectiveness, independent validation and certification rather than allowing one evidence class to stand in for another.

05Assurance posture

Claims remain bounded by their evidence.

SIS develops toward demanding assurance and regulated-system contexts, including Common Criteria / ISO/IEC 15408, DO-178C and DO-333, ISO 26262, IEC 61508, Canadian government security profiles and NIST post-quantum standards.

These are engineering, research and evidence-mapping contexts. They are not blanket claims of certification, accreditation, production authorization, security clearance, independent validation or field effectiveness.
