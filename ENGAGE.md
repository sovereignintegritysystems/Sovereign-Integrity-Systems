# Work with Sovereign Integrity Systems

**PUBLIC_SAFE · authority `NONE` · Saskatchewan, Canada**

Sovereign Integrity Systems (SIS) can help organizations turn a bounded technical, operational, commercial or institutional question into a source-backed map of what is known, what is economically material, what remains uncertain and what to do next.

This page is the GitHub-native engagement front door. It remains usable even when the separate Vercel publication projection is stale or unavailable.

## Good starting questions

- **Are we missing a customer, market, procurement, funding or partnership route?**
- **Where is the avoidable cost, process friction or technical waste?**
- **What evidence would make this technical or commercial decision trustworthy?**
- **Which requirements, obligations, dependencies or currentness changes matter now?**
- **What should we do next, and what would falsify the recommendation?**

## How SIS works

SIS prefers to do useful work before requesting a large internal data package. Where public or client-authorized sources are sufficient, we can first reconstruct the problem, preserve exact source/currentness boundaries, identify plausible opportunities and disconfirmers, and pre-build most or all of the deliverable.

If internal information would materially change the conclusion, we ask for the minimum specific fields needed—for example a capacity figure, cost band, current pipeline overlap or exact technical constraint—rather than broad data access by default.

Typical outputs can include:

- a completed public-source evidence or opportunity report;
- a customized diagnostic and ranked roadmap;
- procurement, funding, customer, supplier or partnership intelligence;
- assurance, evidence and currentness readiness work;
- bounded technical or operational implementation;
- recurring monitoring/currentness where the underlying information changes materially;
- reusable software, checkers or evidence tooling where repeated work justifies productization.

## Commercial terms

There is **no universal SIS rate card and no universal percentage-of-value fee**.

Commercial terms are set case by case from the scope, bounded client economic value, evidence quality, attribution, measurement, implementation dependency, applicable legal or professional constraints, cash timing, collection risk and SIS delivery risk.

Possible structures include a completed prebuilt report, customized report, value-informed fixed or milestone work, recurring intelligence, licence/partner arrangements, or verified outcome participation where the baseline, attribution and measurement basis are sufficiently strong and the exact activity permits it.

Estimated opportunity is not guaranteed value. A proposal is not a contract. Delivery is not proof of an economic outcome. Any outcome-dependent compensation requires an agreed measurement basis before the outcome is used commercially.
