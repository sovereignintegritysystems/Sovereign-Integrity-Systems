# Validation, Independent Review and Problem Discovery

Status: **PUBLIC_SAFE engagement intake**. This page invites bounded external evidence and problem discovery. It is not a request for confidential information, a procurement bid, a promise of service, a partnership claim or an assertion that SIS is already validated for the listed environments.

Sovereign Integrity Systems is looking for a small number of serious external interactions that can falsify or strengthen its work more efficiently than additional internal analysis.

## 1. Technical criticism

SIS welcomes independent technical critique of public-safe methods, especially:

- evidence-state non-promotion and currentness;
- high-assurance software evidence and reproducibility;
- failure-state and degraded-mode semantics;
- assurance of constrained, disconnected or edge systems;
- measurement, uncertainty and evidence provenance;
- AI/model evidence governance.

Useful responses identify a concrete counterexample, competing method, missing prior art, incorrect assumption or operational cost.

## 2. Product and operator problem discovery

SIS is interested in speaking with operators, engineers, maintainers, technical buyers and integrators who work in environments where any of these problems are material:

- evidence for what actually ran, where and under which configuration;
- continuity when cloud/network/provider dependencies are unavailable;
- trustworthy provenance across software, sensors or records;
- safe bounded authority and human approval in automated workflows;
- cryptographic migration in constrained or long-lived systems;
- validation of high-consequence digital or physical/digital systems.

The first discussion can remain at the problem level. SIS does not need proprietary network diagrams, credentials, classified information, personal data or procurement-sensitive material to determine whether a useful seam exists.

## 3. Independent testing and measurement

SIS is also interested in facilities and researchers capable of producing genuinely independent evidence in areas such as:

- target/HIL and hostile-environment testing;
- materials and physical measurement;
- calibration, uncertainty and reproducibility;
- fault injection and reliability testing;
- human-in-the-loop/operator evaluation;
- independent cryptographic or software-assurance review.

The intended outcome is a bounded evidence plan with explicit IP, data, safety, publication and independence conditions—not a generic endorsement.

## 4. Standards, research and policy participation

SIS is open to contribution-oriented participation where its public-safe methods are relevant to an active technical, research, standards or policy question. Participation or contact should not be interpreted as committee standing, institutional endorsement or policy influence unless the relevant external body establishes that status.

## 5. What SIS will measure

SIS is deliberately trying to replace vanity metrics with stronger evidence. Useful external outcomes include:

- a problem hypothesis is rejected;
- a user or buyer confirms a problem is important enough to act on;
- an independent reviewer finds a material defect;
- a method is reproduced or fails to reproduce;
- a target or physical test changes a claim;
- an external implementation reuses a method or interface;
- an institution cites, adopts or meaningfully challenges a contribution.

Negative results are valuable and will not be converted into positive marketing claims.
